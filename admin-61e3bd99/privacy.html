Privacy Policy

Abyss: Worldheart · Viral Media Group LLC · Effective October 22, 2026

This policy explains what information Abyss: Worldheart ("the game") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The game is operated by Viral Media Group LLC, Illinois, USA ("we", "us").

Playing without an account

You can play without signing in. Your progress is then saved only in your own browser on your device. We do not receive it.

What we collect if you sign in

  • Email and password sign-in: your email address. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider and stored only in hashed form; we never see it.
  • Google sign-in: the basic profile information Google shares when you choose to sign in: your email address, name and profile picture. We use only the email address to identify your account.
  • Game save data: your game progress (characters, items, currencies, stage clears, settings) and a short summary of it (such as your account rank and profile name), so it can be restored on any device you sign in on.
  • Profile name: the name you choose in the game.

How we use it

Only to sign you in, store your save, and keep it in sync between your devices. We do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Service providers

  • Supabase stores accounts and cloud saves.
  • Google provides "Sign in with Google" if you choose it.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the game's website and may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and cloud save until you ask us to delete them.

Deleting your data

Email viralprodbusiness@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete your account. We will delete your account and cloud save. Saves kept in your own browser can be removed by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Connections to our servers are encrypted, and each account can only read and write its own save. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
